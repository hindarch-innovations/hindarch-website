What exactly does HindArch build, and why does it matter?

HindArch builds AI-powered software tools for semiconductor chip design engineers. Our platform covers three critical phases of the design lifecycle — prediction, RTL generation, and verification. Traditional chip design is heavily dependent on expensive, time-consuming EDA tool flows. HindArch eliminates these bottlenecks by letting engineers work smarter at every stage, without being locked into legacy tool dependencies.

What are the three products HindArch is building?

HindPred enables tool-free early prediction of chip metrics directly from RTL — giving design teams early insight without running full synthesis flows. HindGen converts natural language specifications into RTL code and automates module scaffolding, accelerating hardware design from idea to implementation. HindVer auto-generates UVM testbenches from natural language, reducing the manual effort that makes verification one of the most time-consuming parts of chip design.

Are the products available yet, and what has been validated?

All three products are currently under active development and will be launching soon. HindPred has validated tool-free RTL prediction, speed and throughput analysis, and multimeric co-prediction. HindGen has validated natural-language to RTL conversion and automated module scaffolding. HindVer has validated natural-language to UVM testbench generation. Features like explainability, EDA integration, and cross-platform support are on the active roadmap.

Who are HindArch’s tools built for?

HindArch tools are designed for semiconductor chip design engineers — RTL designers, VLSI architects, and verification engineers working in semiconductor companies, fabless design houses, and research labs. Our tools speak the language of the engineer: RTL, UVM, EDA flows, and testbenches. We are India-built and globally competitive, solving a real infrastructure problem in the chip design workflow.
